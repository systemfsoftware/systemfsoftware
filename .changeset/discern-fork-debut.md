Add @systemfsoftware/discern: uncertainty-aware semantic pattern matching and control flow over Effect's `DecisionModel`. Classify, rate and probability decisions become patterns with a three-valued verdict — `Match`, `Miss`, `Uncertain` — that compose with `when`, `and`, `or` and `not`, drive an exhaustive `match`/`case`, and carry an inspectable plan, trace, replay and cached observation store. The `@systemfsoftware/discern/procedure` entry routes a request between named procedures and reports `Uncertain` rather than picking a near-tie.
