title: Copy for Younna surfaces is written by Gemini from the brief's copy source, and builders place it without authoring it
applies_when:
  - a UI unit adds or changes headings, body text, labels or empty-state text on a Younna surface
  - a builder edits copy in a component, a page or a story
  - writing or reviewing the order for a landing page, an app shell or a signed-in screen
tags: [ui-craft, copy, gemini, brief]
---

Copy written by the model that builds layout comes out as the average of its training set: metaphor taglines, triads and inflated adjectives. Copy for Younna surfaces is therefore written by Gemini (OpenRouter `google/gemini-3.8-flash`, called through `~/.local/state/fleet-ledger/gemini-or.ts`) from the brief's copy source file, the verbatim phrases of real users. Claude and GPT build layout and motion only. They place the copy they are given and do not author or rewrite it. A copy change is a new Gemini call, never a builder edit.

Evidence. The founder called the landing copy "complete AI slop" on 2026-10-23, and the same rule is checked after the build by the blind-read clarity gate of `an-independent-judge-gates-the-merge`.

Gate: `review` — Does every commit that adds or changes on-screen copy cite the Gemini call record that produced it, and is the source of that call the brief's copy source file?
